BusLive for Android

Free, no ads, no tracking. Live map, arrivals, trip planner, live directions with get-off alerts, widget.

Open the file on your phone and allow "Install unknown apps".

What the map shows

Colored marker = live bus GPS (line number, arrow = heading). Faded = older than 3 min.

LIVE · estimate = arrival estimated from a live bus on the way. Plain time = timetable.

Data: Open Bus Stride (Hasadna) from the Ministry of Transport SIRI/GTFS. Live data is ~1–4 min behind. Map © OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors.
